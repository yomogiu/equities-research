# Review procedure

Compare originals and renderings directly; fluency is insufficient. For a full
document, review every translated segment. If only a sample is reviewed, record
reviewed/unreviewed spans and label review partial. For selected evidence, check
surrounding text for material qualifications or reversed meaning.

Use four criteria, recording source/translation offsets for findings:

- **Meaning:** preserve negation, modality, tense, conditions, comparisons, causal
  claims, technical terms and unresolved ambiguity.
- **Numbers, units and periods:** preserve every material value, sign, scale, currency,
  denominator, date and reporting basis. Check tables with headers and footnotes.
  Explain intentional conversions.
- **Attribution:** distinguish speakers, companies, customers and third-party claims;
  label translator notes and interpretations.
- **Coverage:** cover requested passages/sections, footnotes and relevant visual text;
  disclose omissions and unreadability. A complete extraction can still omit PDF content.

Return actionable findings rather than stylistic rewrites. Errors changing a claim,
number, unit, period, attribution or scope are material. Recheck corrected artifacts.
Do not pass unresolved material errors; stop after two repair rounds with blocked or
unresolved findings rather than cycling indefinitely.

## Financial checks

- Distinguish fiscal/calendar year, quarter/year-to-date, and period/publication date.
- Distinguish revenue, bookings, billings, backlog, profit and cash flow; do not map
  unfamiliar accounting labels to familiar but different metrics.
- Preserve consolidated/standalone, GAAP/IFRS/adjusted, reported/constant currency,
  and forecast/historical distinctions.
- Check decimal/thousands separators, negative parentheses and table-header magnitude
  labels. 億 denotes 100 million in Japanese and Chinese; retain table-wide units
  when translating a single row.
- Preserve uncertainty, conditional outlook and Q&A attribution. An analyst's
  question is not management guidance.
- Retain original number/unit when presentation changes. Mechanical digit matching
  can flag problems but does not validate semantic equivalence.

Do not add valuation or investment recommendations unless separately requested.
