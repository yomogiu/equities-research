# Research contract

Collect the smallest evidence set that can support the requested decision, then broaden where evidence is thin, conflicting, or valuation-sensitive.

## Source hierarchy

1. Regulators and exchanges: filings, ownership forms, prospectuses, enforcement records
2. Company sources: earnings releases, presentations, transcripts, product documentation, pricing, governance materials
3. Primary industry sources: competitors' filings, government statistics, standards bodies, clinical registries, patents, procurement records
4. Trusted market-data and estimate providers
5. High-quality secondary reporting and specialist research

Use social media, forums, and generated summaries only as leads. Verify decision-relevant claims elsewhere.

## Evidence fields

For every material number or claim, retain:

- `claim_or_metric`
- `value`, `units`, and `currency`
- `period` and `as_of`
- `source_title`, `source_url`, `source_type`, and access date
- reported, calculated, estimated, assumed, or analyst judgment
- calculation or transformation
- confidence and conflicts

## Common analytical spine

### 1. Security and capitalization

Verify price timestamp, basic and diluted shares, options/RSUs/convertibles, market cap, cash, investments, debt, leases, pensions, minority interests, preferred equity, and enterprise value. Reconcile corporate actions and units.

### 2. Business and industry

Explain products, customers, buying reason, pricing, revenue model, distribution, geography, concentration, competitors, substitutes, value chain, regulation, moat, cyclicality, and industry structure. Try or inspect the product when feasible.

### 3. Historical economics

Default full-underwrite history: five fiscal years plus eight quarters when available. Collect revenue, organic growth, gross profit, operating expenses, operating income, taxes, net income, EPS, diluted shares, working capital, capex, operating cash flow, free cash flow, debt, and relevant segment/KPI data.

### 4. Earnings quality

Reconcile reported and adjusted results. Inspect revenue recognition, stock compensation, restructuring, impairments, acquisition accounting, capitalized costs, working-capital timing, tax normalization, dilution, maintenance versus growth capex, and recurring cash conversion.

### 5. Capital allocation and governance

Assess reinvestment returns, acquisitions, dividends, repurchases, debt policy, dilution, insider ownership, incentives, related parties, board structure, governance controversies, and management's record versus prior targets.

### 6. Forecast drivers

Forecast from operating drivers rather than extrapolating headline growth. Show price, volume, mix, customers, capacity, utilization, margins, reinvestment, share count, and financing where material. Tie forecasts to an explicit base period.

### 7. Valuation and what is priced in

Choose methods appropriate to the economics: DCF, earnings or FCF multiple, EV/EBITDA, asset value, SOTP, residual income, dividend model, or probability-adjusted asset value. Use at least one cross-check. Backsolve the operating assumptions required by the current price.

### 8. Variant perception and path

State the market's apparent view, the analyst's differing view, why the gap exists, what closes it, catalyst timing, ownership/positioning when sourced, and what could leave the security cheap or expensive indefinitely.

### 9. Risk and falsification

Describe downside mechanisms, not generic risk labels. Define observable facts that would invalidate each thesis pillar. Include financing, liquidity, accounting, regulation, competition, technology, customer, management, and terminal-value risks as relevant.

### 10. Scenarios and action

Build bull, base, and bear cases with internally consistent operating assumptions, valuation, probability only when requested, return/skew, and signposts. Distinguish research posture from portfolio action.

## Mode depth

- `quick_screen`: identity, Big Six, business, recent results, key debate, rough valuation, disqualifiers, and next research step
- `full_underwrite`: complete common spine, sector overlay, scenarios, source register, and valuation support
- `position_review`: thesis change, evidence since entry, valuation, catalysts, sizing constraints, and add/hold/trim/exit conditions
- `event_update`: event facts, expectation versus outcome, model/thesis impact, valuation change, next catalyst, and action implications
