# Sector overlays

Load only the relevant sections. Mixed companies may require multiple overlays.

## Software and internet

- ARR/revenue composition, organic growth, NRR/GRR, bookings/RPO, customer count and cohorts
- Seat, usage, transaction, advertising, or consumption drivers
- Gross-margin structure, hosting costs, sales efficiency, CAC payback, churn, and cohort contribution
- Stock compensation, dilution, capitalization of development, and adjusted-margin quality
- Platform dependence, switching costs, data advantage, ecosystem, bundling, and AI substitution

## Semiconductors and hardware

- End-market and product mix, units, ASP, content, utilization, inventory, backlog, and channel digestion
- Fabless/foundry/IDM model, node, yield, capacity commitments, packaging, geographic and supplier concentration
- Design wins, product cycles, customer concentration, export controls, and technology road map
- Cyclical versus structural growth, acquisition effects, capex intensity, and normalized margins

## Consumer, retail, and restaurants

- Traffic, ticket, units, same-store sales, store cohorts, square footage, e-commerce, and channel mix
- Gross margin, markdowns, shrink, labor, occupancy, logistics, advertising, and customer acquisition
- Inventory turns, working capital, franchise economics, store-level returns, and saturation
- Brand strength, private-label/substitution risk, consumer income sensitivity, and promotional intensity

## Industrials, aerospace, and transportation

- Orders, backlog quality, book-to-bill, price/cost, volume, mix, utilization, aftermarket, and installed base
- Capacity, lead times, supply constraints, program accounting, warranties, and contract structure
- Maintenance versus growth capex, working capital, cyclicality, customer concentration, and government exposure

## Banks and specialty finance

- Asset and deposit mix, deposit beta, NIM, loan growth, credit costs, delinquencies, charge-offs, and reserves
- Capital ratios, liquidity, duration, securities losses, wholesale funding, and regulatory constraints
- Tangible book value, ROTCE, normalized earnings, stress losses, and dilution risk

## Insurance

- Premium growth, pricing, retention, combined ratio, reserve development, catastrophe exposure, and reinsurance
- Investment portfolio duration/credit, regulatory capital, book value, ROE, and underwriting-cycle position

## Energy and materials

- Production/volume, realized price, benchmark exposure, differentials, grades, decline rates, reserves, and inventory
- Unit cash cost, sustaining/growth capex, royalties, transport, hedges, project economics, and jurisdiction
- Balance-sheet resilience and free cash flow across commodity scenarios; avoid valuing peak margins as permanent

## Biotechnology and pharmaceuticals

- Indication, mechanism, target population, standard of care, administration burden, differentiation, and competition
- Trial design, endpoints, effect size, durability, safety, statistical strength, subgroup risk, and external validity
- Development stage, regulatory path, probability of success, patent/exclusivity, manufacturing, reimbursement, and launch
- Patient funnel, diagnosis, penetration, price, dosing, adherence, peak sales, costs, cash runway, and financing/dilution
- Build probability-adjusted asset value; do not value a pipeline from unadjusted peak sales

## Medical devices and healthcare services

- Procedure or patient volume, reimbursement, utilization, installed base, consumables, physician workflow, and outcomes
- Site mix, payer mix, labor intensity, regulation, recalls, purchasing groups, and customer concentration

## REITs and real estate

- Same-property NOI, occupancy, rent spreads, lease duration, tenant concentration, development pipeline, and cap rates
- AFFO quality, recurring capex, leverage, debt maturities, fixed/floating mix, secured debt, and NAV sensitivity

## Marketplaces and payments

- GMV/TPV, take rate, active users/merchants, frequency, cohort retention, cross-border/mix, and loss/fraud rates
- Network effects, disintermediation, incentives, payment costs, regulation, working capital, and credit exposure

## Roll-ups and acquisitive businesses

- Separate organic growth from acquired growth and quantify purchase accounting
- Track acquisition multiples, financing, integration, synergies, ROIC, leverage, covenant headroom, and serial equity issuance
- Test whether per-share value creation survives normalized taxes, stock compensation, restructuring, and maintenance capex
