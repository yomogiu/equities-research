# Output contract

Lead with the investment answer. Keep description subordinate to the decision.

## Required opening

State:

- Security, analysis date, price as-of, and reporting currency
- Mode and underwriting stage: `screen`, `watchlist`, `preliminary_underwrite`, or `underwritten_view`
- One-sentence research posture
- Decision hinge: the fact or forecast that most changes the conclusion
- Evidence confidence and the largest unresolved gap

## Core sections

1. **Answer and variant perception** — what the market appears to expect, what the analysis sees differently, and why it matters
2. **Business economics** — how the company makes money, why customers buy, durability, and industry position
3. **Financial and operating evidence** — normalized history, key KPIs, earnings quality, cash conversion, balance sheet, and capital allocation
4. **Forecast and valuation** — operating drivers, current-price backsolve, primary valuation, cross-check, and sensitivity
5. **Bull/base/bear** — coherent assumptions, valuation/return, signposts, and probability only when requested
6. **Catalysts and path** — what can close the gap and on what timeline
7. **Risks and falsifiers** — mechanisms, observable disconfirmers, and mitigation or lack thereof
8. **Research gaps and next work** — missing evidence and the next highest-value diligence step
9. **Sources and assumptions** — compact source register, data cut-off, conflicts, and material calculations

## Writing rules

- Separate reported facts, company claims, consensus, calculations, assumptions, and judgment.
- Cite the complete claim or table row; avoid citation fragments inside ticker, period, price, or metric names.
- Use tables for comparable periods, scenarios, and explicit mappings. Do not add decorative charts.
- State what would change the view. Avoid vague “monitor execution” conclusions.
- Do not provide a target price without source-backed capitalization and forecast assumptions.
- Do not call a security investable merely because evidence quality is high; confidence and attractiveness are different.

## Quick-screen close

End a quick screen with one of:

- Reject and why
- Watchlist plus the trigger for renewed work
- Promote to full underwrite plus the exact evidence required
- Trade setup requiring separate risk and timing analysis

Never end a quick screen with an unsupported buy or sell recommendation.
