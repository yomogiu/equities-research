# Common report evidence

The finite runner supplies the same source view and complete call to both author
and independent reviewer. Annual MD&A, financial statements and notes remain
present even when an index repeats Item 8 near the end of a filing. Large SEC
annual views retain every captured table page and the complete substantive Item 7
and Item 8 sections; other annual pages may be omitted explicitly. A substantive
Item 7 -> Item 8 -> Item 9 chain is required; unrecognized layouts retain full text.
An incorporated-by-reference layout instead requires an appended audit heading,
consolidated balance sheets and an explicit next-exhibit page boundary. Large
quarterly views can omit a substantive Item 1A risk section while retaining the
complete financial sections, legal proceedings and every captured table. These
boundaries reject table-of-contents entries and always preserve complete originals.
This is an earnings update, not a full annual risk review. Complete originals and
all extracted financial cells remain frozen. A
correction and final check reuse this compact original view instead of expanding
document citations into uncompressed duplicate filings. No new source search occurs
after authorship. Frozen sources, hashes and numerical extraction are unchanged.

For positional PDF tables, the author receives every selectable row ID printed
beside its original source line, avoiding a separate mental row-number join.
Short P-number table aliases bind exactly to the frozen original table IDs. They
are expanded before selection validation, with an audit record. Unused dictionary
strings and duplicate row-address lists are omitted from the model view.
Original page text is supplied once, with
horizontal whitespace compacted and blank layout lines omitted. The published
table uses the original selected lines in a scrollable preformatted block, keeping
printed year, unit, duration and sparse-column alignment. An otherwise empty prior
page containing a detached statement heading is retained as quoted context;
another statement's integer-valued data cannot qualify as a detached heading.
Selected excerpts omit unrelated trailing prose and unselected ambiguous data
lines. Review checks that available requested core figures are actually present
in the selected table rows, rather than supplied only in the narrative. No
currency, scale, accounting period or financial amount is inferred by rendering.
The model receives source text in fenced blocks rather than JSON strings nested
inside another serialized message. Task metadata remains structured and bound to
the same artifact; source characters are unchanged apart from layout whitespace.

Known optional metric gaps use the same metric vocabulary as selections. Required
coverage, unknown-ID rejection, source citations and selected-versus-missing checks
remain enforced. Missing normalization is distinct from missing disclosure.

Citation formatting is resolved before output validation. Exact bound full-document
IDs, table/row IDs and unique hexadecimal abbreviations map to the supplied original
scope, with a raw-to-resolved audit record. Abbreviations require at least 16
unchanged hex characters; an interior elision must retain at least six characters
at both ends and omit no more than eight. Changed characters, ambiguous matches and
unsupplied sources remain invalid. Claim support still requires independent review.

A correction preserves Q&A that already passed independent review when no blocking
finding concerns Q&A or cites one of its exchanges/turns. Its unrequested rewrite is
retained in the raw response but not applied to the candidate, with a separate
preservation audit. A real Q&A finding still permits repair. Final independent
review and the exact-candidate publication checks remain required.

Reader-facing scope notes describe source coverage, missing materials and reporting
periods. Preparer instructions are not published. A report-only run does not show an
empty call-question heading, and original table excerpts do not repeat internal
normalization-gap notices before every section.

Full-year EPS guidance ranges may enter the supplemental valuation inventory only
when quoted from the bound call with an explicit annual scope, fiscal label,
accounting basis, call date and recognized matching USD security. The renderer
computes the range and midpoint forward P/E using Decimal arithmetic and retains
the historical quote and guidance dates. Quarterly EPS is never annualized.
Independent review must still verify accounting, share and ADR basis.

Document-defined fiscal labels need not share a year with calendar endpoints.
An exact quoted endpoint can establish a fiscal report date when it matches the
original opening period and precedes the matched call. Contradictions still fail.

Report-only collection recognizes simplified and traditional Chinese statement
headings and quarter labels. Issuer-linked CNINFO disclosure archives use their
public stock/organization-scoped query, with bounded pages and fresh PDF retrieval.
Returned issuer IDs, dates, original titles and download URLs are checked. Summary,
forecast and unrelated financial-adviser reports are not quarterly statements.
The caller retains the archive responses and request bindings. This is upstream
discovery, never a post-draft repair or an older-period substitution.

All live tests remain new immutable runs. A historical failure is never edited or
re-entered. New explicitly authorized retests bind historical outcome and cost
receipts, retain the standard company ceiling, independent review, one correction
and exact private publication, and do not reset the Terra campaign ledger.
For a proven preflight stop before authoring or review was dispatched, a newly
authorized component test may reuse authenticated completed stages against their
identical source case. It never replays those paid stages. A completed author is
hash-bound to its raw response and source case; its independent review must use a
new session. Every earlier cost reduces the remaining original test allowance.
Recurring automation remains paused during validation and after successful tests
until separately authorized.
