# Missing review passage proposal contract

`authenticated-missing-review-passage-proposal-v1` is an explicit offline
eligibility and typed-proposal validator. It does not change default replay,
reserve a model job, create a continuation or accept a report.

Eligibility requires a clean official original frozen verifier, four exact
closed original roles, original routes and prompts, complete original review
inspection and the exact failed adjudication. Every retained decision is
individually checked by the ordinary adjudicator. Only one or two factual
analysis decisions whose passage lists are empty may be targeted. Other defects
and reopened units fail closed.

A proposal may supply only unique original indexed passage IDs for every target.
It cannot alter reasons, candidate prose, quotations, existing citations,
criteria, other decisions or original output bytes. Identifiers and reviewer
prose are never mapping authority. The source adjudicator must inspect complete
original evidence and support the unchanged finding. `validate_fresh_proposal`
uses ordinary complete review coverage over the entire candidate and selected
bindings; it grants no acceptance.

Not implemented or authorized by this module: launcher/request authentication,
canonical single-reservation accounting, model activation, source qualification,
funding admission, coordinator registry integration or frozen successor
preparation. Those remain mandatory before use. Any future activated recovery
must preserve all original charged sessions, models, correction/resume limits
and accounting, and must fund the entire remaining correction and NEW independent
FULL review with full output reservations. Accepted canary plus exact remote
report proof is required before broader adoption. Offline eligibility alone is
not an activated report repair.
